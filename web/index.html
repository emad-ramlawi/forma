OPTIONAL STEP 3 · CERTIFICATE SIGNING

Add E-signature

Use a certificate containing your private key, or create a self-signed identity locally.

A self-signed certificate does not independently verify your identity. Recipients must explicitly trust it. Valid for one year.

Enter the certificate password, or leave blank if it has none.

A certificate signature detects document changes. Forma does not verify issuer trust or revocation, and does not add a trusted timestamp. Acceptance depends on the recipient.

The exported PDF is a fixed image-based copy, including your filled fields, QR code, and any placed visual signatures. The certificate signature appears in your PDF reader’s signature panel. Other signatures already in the source are not preserved.

Keys and passwords are used locally in memory and are not saved by Forma. If you created an identity, you may download its encrypted certificate for reuse; keep it and its password private.
