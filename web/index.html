FORMA 0.1.2

Small tools. Useful work.

Offline form filling and visual signatures for Linux.

  • PDF.js 6.4.299 renders standard forms and supported XFA forms.
  • Editable copies retain PDF form data. Signed export saves one fixed, image-based PDF at 144 dpi. Save an editable copy separately if you want one.
  • PPTC 042 (08-2026) QR codes update as you fill and appear in both saved copies and signed exports. General Adobe XFA scripts and other barcode calculations are not executed.
  • Signatures are handwritten images, not certificate-based digital signatures. Check the form's own signing instructions before submitting it.
  • Both save actions open Save As so you choose the folder and filename. Existing files and originals are never overwritten.
